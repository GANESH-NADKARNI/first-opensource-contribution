---

## 👋 What is this?

This is a beginner-friendly project. You add a small card with your name and links. That's it. You just contributed to open source.

---

## 🪜 Steps to Contribute

### Step 1 — Fork this repo

Click the **Fork** button at the top right of this page.
This creates your own copy of the project.

---

### Step 2 — Open `index.html` on GitHub

In your forked repo, click on `index.html` to open it.
Then click the **pencil icon ✏️** (top right) to edit it.

---

### Step 3 — Find this line

```
<!-- ADD YOUR CARD BELOW THIS LINE -->
```

---

### Step 4 — Paste your card right below it

Copy this template and fill in your details:

```html
<div class="card-wrap">
  <div class="card" data-avatar="https://link-to-your-photo.jpg">
    <div class="card-shine"></div>
    <div class="card-holo"></div>
    <div class="card-edge"></div>
    <div class="card-number"></div>
    <div class="card-suit"></div>
    <div class="card-top">
      <div class="avatar-wrap">
        <div class="avatar-ring"></div>
        <div class="avatar-ring-inner"></div>
        <div class="avatar"></div>
      </div>
      <div class="card-name">Your Name</div>
      <div class="card-divider"></div>
    </div>
    <div class="card-links">
      <a href="https://linkedin.com/in/your-profile" target="_blank" class="badge linkedin"><span class="badge-dot"></span>LinkedIn</a>
      <a href="https://github.com/your-username" target="_blank" class="badge github"><span class="badge-dot"></span>GitHub</a>
      <a href="https://leetcode.com/your-username" target="_blank" class="badge leetcode"><span class="badge-dot"></span>LeetCode</a>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>
```

✅ `data-avatar` is optional — paste any public image URL to show your photo
✅ If you skip `data-avatar`, your initials appear automatically
✅ Delete any `<a>` line for links you don't have
✅ Do not change anything else — the card number, suit, and effects are all automatic

---

### Step 5 — Commit the change

Scroll down, write a message like:

```
add card for Your Name
```

Click **Commit changes**.

---

### Step 6 — Open a Pull Request

Go back to your forked repo.
Click **Contribute → Open Pull Request**.
Click **Create Pull Request**.

---

### Step 7 — Wait for merge 🎉

Once approved, your card will appear live on the website!

---

## 🚫 Do Not Touch

| File | Reason |
|------|--------|
| `style.css` | Controls all the design |
| `main.js` | Controls search and card numbers |

Only edit `index.html` and only inside the marked section.

---

## 📋 Rules

- One card per person
- Only your own information
- Do not change anyone else's card
